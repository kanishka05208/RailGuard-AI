## RailGuard AI

Team: ElevateX
College: Panimalar Engineering College

## About the Project

RailGuard AI is an idea we developed for a hackathon at Eshwari Engineering College. Our project focuses on elevator guide-rail monitoring and how AI could help identify problems before they become serious.

The main idea is to monitor the rail alignment and vibration during elevator operation. If the system notices something unusual, it could alert the maintenance team.

## Why We Chose This Problem

Guide-rail problems such as misalignment, loosened brackets, and abnormal vibration can develop gradually. These issues may affect elevator ride quality and reliability.

We wanted to explore whether a sensor-based system could help detect these changes early and support better maintenance.

## How Our Idea Works

Our proposed system follows a simple flow:

**Sense → Analyze → Detect → Alert → Maintenance**

 **Sense:** Collect alignment and vibration data using sensors.
 **Analyze:** Compare the data with what is considered normal.
 **Detect:** Identify unusual patterns.
 **Alert:** Generate an early warning.
 **Maintenance:** A proposed dashboard and chatbot could help guide the technician.
